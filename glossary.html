Vedic Astrology Glossary

Plain-English definitions of the Sanskrit and Hindi terms you will meet in a Vedic astrology reading.

Ayanamsa
The angular difference (about 24°) between the tropical and sidereal zodiacs. Lahiri is the most widely used value.
Bhava
A house of the birth chart. There are twelve, each linked to areas of life such as career, marriage or home.
Dasha
A planetary period. The Vimshottari system divides life into planetary periods totalling 120 years. Each major period is a Mahadasha and its sub-periods are Antardashas.
Dosha
A traditional term for an unfavourable chart condition, for example Manglik Dosh. Its strength depends on the whole chart.
Gochar
Transit: the current movement of the planets through the zodiac, read against your birth chart.
Graha
A planet or planetary influence. Jyotish uses nine: Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Rahu and Ketu.
Guna Milan
The traditional 36-point system of matching two charts for marriage compatibility.
Jyotish
The Sanskrit name for Vedic astrology, meaning 'science of light'.
Kundli (Janam Kundli)
A birth chart, calculated from your date, time and place of birth.
Lagna
The Rising sign (ascendant): the sign on the eastern horizon at birth. It sets the twelve houses.
Manglik Dosh
A chart condition when Mars is in certain houses (commonly 1, 4, 7, 8 or 12). Often considered in marriage matching.
Mantra
A sacred syllable or phrase repeated as a spiritual practice. Sometimes suggested as an optional remedy.
Muhurat
An auspicious time window chosen for an important event such as a wedding, house entry or business launch.
Nakshatra
One of the 27 lunar mansions of the sidereal zodiac, each divided into four padas.
Panchang
The Hindu almanac, listing tithi, weekday, nakshatra, yoga and karana for each day.
Pandit
A traditional Indian title for a learned scholar. 'Panditji' adds the respectful '-ji'.
Puja
A ritual of worship or offering, sometimes performed as a remedy or for an occasion.
Rahu and Ketu
The lunar nodes: points where the Moon's orbit crosses the Sun's path. Treated as shadow planets in Jyotish.
Rashi (Moon sign)
The zodiac sign the Moon occupied at birth. Central to Vedic interpretation.
Sade Sati
The roughly 7.5-year period when Saturn passes through the signs around your Moon sign.
Varshaphal
An annual forecast, based on the chart cast for each birthday.
