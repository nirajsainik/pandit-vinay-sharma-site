How to Choose a Genuine Astrologer

By Pandit Vinay Kumar Sharma · Published 2026-10-25 · 3 min read

Astrology can be a thoughtful source of guidance, but a few unscrupulous people exploit worried clients. Use these signs to find an honest consultation.

In this article
  • Green flags
  • Red flags
  • Questions worth asking
  • Astrology is guidance, not a substitute
  • Our approach
  • FAQ

Green flags

  1. They listen first. A good astrologer asks about your situation before giving answers.
  2. They explain the chart. You should understand the reasoning, not just hear conclusions.
  3. They speak in possibilities. Honest guidance talks about tendencies and timing, not certainty.
  4. Transparent fees. The cost is discussed clearly and upfront, with no hidden add-ons.
  5. Reasonable remedies. Suggestions are simple and optional, not costly or urgent.

Red flags

  1. Guaranteed results. Nobody can promise to fix a relationship, win a case or change someone's mind. 'Guaranteed in 24 hours' claims are a warning sign.
  2. Fear tactics. Statements such as 'a curse is on your family' used to push you into paying are manipulative.
  3. Pressure to pay immediately, or repeated requests for more money for 'special puja'.

Questions worth asking

  • What does this chart indicate, and why?
  • What are the limits of this reading?
  • Is the remedy necessary or optional, and what does it cost?

Astrology is guidance, not a substitute

Serious medical, legal, financial or mental-health concerns need qualified professionals. A responsible astrologer will say so. Astrology works best as an additional lens, not as the only basis for important decisions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can an astrologer really guarantee a solution?

No. Honest astrologers offer guidance and perspective, not guarantees.

How much should an astrologer charge?

Fees vary by experience and consultation type. What matters is that they are transparent and agreed upfront.

Are online consultations reliable?

They can be, provided the astrologer is genuine, explains the chart clearly and has your accurate birth details.
