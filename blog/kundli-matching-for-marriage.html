Kundli Matching for Marriage: What Guna Milan Means

By Pandit Vinay Kumar Sharma · 25 Oct 2026 · 3 min read

Before many Indian marriages, families compare the horoscopes of the boy and girl. The traditional method is called Ashtakoot Guna Milan, and it produces a score out of 36. Here is what that score does, and does not, tell you.

The 8 koots and their points

Guna Milan compares the Moon's position (nakshatra and rashi) in both charts across eight categories:

  • Varna (1 point): temperament and spiritual compatibility
  • Vashya (2): mutual attraction and influence
  • Tara (3): health and well-being
  • Yoni (4): physical and instinctive compatibility
  • Graha Maitri (5): mental compatibility and friendship
  • Gana (6): nature (divine, human or demonic temperament)
  • Bhakoot (7): emotional bond and family welfare
  • Nadi (8): health and genetic compatibility

These add up to 36 points.

What is a good score?

Traditionally, 18 out of 36 is the minimum for a match to be considered acceptable. Scores of 24 and above are generally regarded as good, and 32 and above as excellent. Nadi and Bhakoot carry the most weight, so a low score in those two is usually examined closely.

Beyond the score

Guna Milan looks only at the Moon. A thorough match also compares the full charts: the 7th house and its lord, the strength of Venus and Jupiter, the Manglik status of both partners, and the running dasha periods. A high score with a troubled 7th house deserves attention, while a modest score with strong supporting factors can still be workable.

Using matching wisely

Kundli matching is a traditional tool for guidance. It should sit alongside real-life factors such as values, family, education, health, communication and mutual respect, not replace them. A good astrologer will explain any concerns calmly and suggest practical remedies instead of creating fear.

What you need for a kundli match

You will need the full name, date, exact time and place of birth for both partners. With these, a consultation can cover the Guna score, Manglik check and a broader compatibility reading.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum Guna Milan score for marriage?

18 out of 36 is traditionally considered the minimum acceptable score.

Is a score of 36 necessary?

No. A perfect score is rare and is not required. The overall chart compatibility matters more.

What if Nadi dosha is present?

Nadi dosha is taken seriously in tradition, but astrologers check for cancellation conditions before drawing conclusions.
