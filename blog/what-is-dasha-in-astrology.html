What Is Dasha in Astrology? Vimshottari Dasha Explained

By Pandit Vinay Kumar Sharma · Published 2026-10-25 · 3 min read

If the kundli shows what is possible, the dasha system suggests when. It is the most widely used timing tool in Vedic astrology.

The basic idea

Dasha means 'period'. In the Vimshottari system, your life is divided into planetary periods totalling 120 years. The planet ruling your current period colours the themes you are likely to experience, such as career, relationships or health, according to its placement in your chart.

Mahadasha order and length

  • Ketu: 7 years
  • Venus: 20 years
  • Sun: 6 years
  • Moon: 10 years
  • Mars: 7 years
  • Rahu: 18 years
  • Jupiter: 16 years
  • Saturn: 19 years
  • Mercury: 17 years

The cycle always runs in this order. Which planet you start with depends on the Moon's nakshatra at birth.

Antardasha and sub-periods

Each Mahadasha is divided into smaller Antardashas (sub-periods) ruled by all nine planets in turn. These refine the timing, showing shorter phases of a few months to a few years within the larger period.

How astrologers interpret a dasha

A dasha lord's results depend on its sign, house, strength and relationships in the chart. A strong, well-placed planet tends to deliver its themes more smoothly, while a stressed planet may bring lessons and delays. Transits (the planets' present movement) are then read together with the dasha.

Using dasha for decisions

Dasha can help in choosing the timing of a career move, marriage or major investment, but it is guidance, not a prediction carved in stone. Your own preparation and choices matter just as much.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the Vimshottari Dasha cycle?

The full cycle is 120 years, divided among nine planets.

How do I find my current dasha?

It is calculated from your birth chart using the Moon's nakshatra. An astrologer or kundli software can tell you.

Does a bad dasha mean a bad period?

Not necessarily. Results depend on the planet's placement and strength in your chart.
