Online Vedic Astrology Consultation: What to Expect

By Pandit Vinay Kumar Sharma · 25 Oct 2026 · 3 min read

You do not need to be in India to consult a Vedic astrologer. Online sessions work well for clients around the world. Here is what happens and how to prepare.

Step 1: Get in touch

Use the booking form, WhatsApp or phone. Tell us your topic, your country and your time zone so we can propose a suitable time. Panditji's hours are Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 19:00 India Standard Time (UTC+5:30), and we convert them to your local time.

Step 2: Share your birth details

Provide your full name, date, exact time and place (city and country) of birth. If you are unsure about the time, say so. This lets us prepare your chart before the session.

Step 3: The session

Sessions are held over Zoom, phone or WhatsApp video, or in person at the Baglamukhi Temple in Kangra. You can ask for a short session of about 30 minutes or a full session of about 60 minutes. Panditji explains the chart in clear English, covers your questions and suggests simple remedies if relevant.

Step 4: Follow-up

Where requested, you can receive a written summary or PDF report, possibly with a short voice note. Ask in advance whether you may record the session.

How to prepare

  • Write down 2 or 3 clear questions
  • Have your birth details ready, ideally from a birth certificate
  • Choose a quiet place with a stable internet connection
  • Keep an open mind: astrology offers perspective, not guarantees

Fees and payment

Panditji first speaks with you about your concern. Fees are shared after that first conversation, with no obligation, and payment options for international clients are confirmed at that time.

Yes. The chart is calculated from your birth details, so the reading does not depend on your location.

Hours are in IST, and we confirm the time in your local zone before booking.

English. Hindi and Punjabi are also available on request.
