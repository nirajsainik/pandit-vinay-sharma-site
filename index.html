New to Vedic Astrology?

Vedic astrology (Jyotish) is the traditional astrology of India. It looks like Western astrology, but it uses a different zodiac, so your signs may be different.

TopicWestern astrologyVedic astrology (Jyotish)
ZodiacTropical, tied to the seasonsSidereal, tied to the fixed stars
Main focusSun signRising sign (Lagna) and Moon sign
Lunar mansionsNot used27 nakshatras
TimingTransits and progressionsDasha periods plus transits
StyleOften psychologicalOften predictive, with traditional remedies
Why is my Vedic sign different? The two zodiacs have drifted about 24° apart over 2,000 years. A Sun at 5° Aries in a Western chart is at about 11° Pisces in a Vedic chart. If your birthday falls early in a Western sign, your Vedic Sun sign is likely the previous one. Read the full comparison →

Frequently Asked Questions

 

What is Vedic astrology?

Vedic astrology, or Jyotish, is the traditional astrology of India. It uses the sidereal zodiac, the Moon sign and 27 nakshatras, and it times events with dasha periods.

Do I need to be Hindu or Indian to consult?

No. Anyone can have a Vedic reading, whatever their religion, nationality or background.

Can I consult from outside India?

Yes. Sessions are held by Zoom, phone or WhatsApp video, and your chart is calculated from your birth details wherever you were born.

What do I need to share?

Your full name, date of birth, exact time of birth and place of birth (city and country). If you do not know the time, say so and Panditji will advise.

How much does a consultation cost?

Panditji first speaks with you about your concern. Fees are discussed after that first conversation, with no obligation.

Do you guarantee results?

No. Astrology offers guidance and perspective, and outcomes depend on many factors, including your own decisions.
